# ItemOffer

Hand an item to someone without dropping it first.

- Press the offer key (J by default, G in the SS13 preset) or use the self verb to hold out the item in your active
  hand. A translucent copy of it floats in front of you; press the key again to lower it.
- Anyone in reach takes it into a free hand by clicking that copy, clicking you with an empty hand, or with the
  Take verb. The offer ends if the item leaves your hand or you go down.
- `WFItemOfferSystem` (shared, predicted) holds the logic; `WFItemOfferIconSystem` and `WFItemOfferOverlay` on the
  client draw the copy and turn clicks on it into a `WFTakeOfferedItemEvent`.
