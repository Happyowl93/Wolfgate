# Narcotics

Street drugs and what they do to a body. Each drug doses a `wfDrugProfile` (a half-life; tiers with speed, traits,
popups and an overlay, grouped into light, heavy and overdose phases; a crash; neuroware strings), and
`WFIntoxicationSystem` ticks the levels, announces phase changes, runs tier effects and crashes, and
grants trait components (damage slowdown, stun resistance, pain numbness, Anvil's damper and its Poison repayment).
`WFDependenceSystem` builds a per-family habit with tolerance and withdrawal; three traits start a character
dependent. The client draws one overlay per family.

Substances are grown (coca, the opium poppy, cannabis pressed into hash) or cooked through graded reactions, and the
solids keep their grade. `WFDeliverySystem` adds powder lines from a baggie and the bong. Products are Class 3
contraband with grade-scaled prices, a sale tax and a sold alert; the Strangers dealer sells cut stock, the Drydock
fence sells full grade and takes drugs as payment, and the uplink chemicals tab lists a few. The Bench has three laws
for it and the Medical guide a player page.

Entry points: `WFDose` and `WFClearIntoxication` (entity effects), `SharedWFIntoxicationSystem` (levels, tiers,
`IsNeurowareHost`), `WFIntoxicationSystem.Tick`, `WFDependenceSystem.Tick`, `WFDeliverySystem`. The design brief, which
also covers `Purity` and `Neuroware`, is [Docs/_WF/Narcotics/DESIGN.md](../../../Docs/_WF/Narcotics/DESIGN.md).
