# ScratchTickets

Mother Lode scratch tickets. A ticket hides nine ores under foil; the holder uses it in hand and drags across the foil
to wear it away, and once 90% is off the rest goes and the ticket is scratched. Three ores alike win the prize printed
in the ticket's legend. Using a scratched winner on any powered bank ATM pays the prize into the user's account (as
cash for an Ironman or anyone without an account) and takes the ticket.

Tickets turn up in maintenance loot, and Scratches-to-Win sells them for 500 thalers at the card table in the
Caelestinus Central bar lounge. He is an ordinary trader (`Traders`), so payment is cash or an ID on his table.

Entry points: `WFScratchTicketSystem` (roll at map init, reveal, ATM payout), `WFScratchTicketComponent` (symbols,
weights and the rolled cells), `WFScratchTicketRules` (the roll), `WFScratchTicketWindow` and `WFScratchFoil` (the
card and its foil).

The outcome is rolled on the server when the ticket spawns and only reaches the client that opens it. The scratching
itself is client-side: the server takes the client's word that the foil is off, which decides nothing. Half-scratched
foil is kept on the client (`WFScratchFoilComponent`) while the ticket stays in view.

Odds and prizes are on the `WFScratchTicket` prototype: weights out of 1000, an average return of about 325 thalers
and a win about one time in three.
