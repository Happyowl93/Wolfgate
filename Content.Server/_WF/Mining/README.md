# Mining

Paces the material economy so the faction voucher ladder ends at about 150 minutes instead of minutes into a round.
Strategic ores (gold, silver, plasma, uranium) drop single ores, about 3 per vein (a third of upstream), lithium drops
0-2 per vein and is the main tuning knob, and sand and chromite only spawn in the Wilds. The scrap processor still smelts strategic sheets, but at 1,500 raw
scrap (lithium 6,000) with no upgrade discount, and lathe upgrade kits don't fit ore processors. Plastitanium is
uncommon in scrap rolls and drone loot, and the crystallizer needs real gas for plastitanium, plasteel and plasma.

The capital ships (Luda, Flyssa, Saturn) sit behind `WFTsfCapitalVouchers` and `WFPdvCapitalVouchers`, so both
factions' top tier costs 585k research points; datafarm boards cost 8 research disks. Hokkaido's gas sale console pays
MMC industrial credits and its bounty board buys PDV composite (`WFMMCBountyPdvComposite`). Prices for fissile and
depleted uranium follow their comments, so the centrifuge is no longer the best credit faucet.

Most of it is prototype data: `research.yml`, `bounties.yml` and `tags.yml`, and marked edits to the upstream ore,
biome, recipe, crystallizer, loot and price prototypes listed below. The one piece of code is
`GasDepositSystem.Wolfgate.cs`: a gas sale console that pays in another currency appraises in it ("N MIC"), a sale
that would pay nothing is refused and leaves the gas in the sale points, and a `MarketModifier` with `hideExamine`
drops the rate line from examine, since the Hokkaido console states its rate in its description.
