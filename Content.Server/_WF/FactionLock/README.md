# FactionLock

One faction per character. The first time a character spawns in a faction job, that faction is saved on the character
and every opposing faction's jobs lock for them: TSF, PDV, MMC, the Viper Group, the USSP, the Underground and the ASF
all oppose each other for now (`WFFactionLockPolicy.Opposes`). The lock belongs to the character, so a player's other
characters are unaffected, and deleting a character deletes its lock. Civilian and high command companies count as
their faction. Neutral jobs, such as Colonial station staff, Contractor and the other ship crew jobs, bind nobody and
stay open to everyone.

Factions and player companies exclude each other. A character who has played a faction can't create, join or apply to
a company, and loses any invitation or application they had when they first play one. A company member can't take a
faction job; leaving the company opens them again.

The lock is a persistent profile flag, `wf-faction:<faction>` (`PersistentProfileSystem`), which client saves can't
change. Admins manage it with `factionunlock <player>`, which lists the player's characters and their locks, and
`factionunlock <player> <slot or character name>`, which clears one character's lock. It works on offline players and
doesn't change which character is selected. A character still spawned in a faction job is bound again by their next
spawn in one.

`WFFactionLockSystem` is the authority: it records the flag the update after a spawn, because Crew Setup can still roll
a spawn back when it finishes, and refuses barred jobs through
`IsJobAllowedEvent`, `StationJobsGetCandidatesEvent` and `GetDisallowedJobsEvent`, which also covers Crew Setup. The
client's copy of a profile only refreshes on lobby join, so `WFCompanyState.PlayedFactions` carries the locks at once,
and `WFFactionLockClientSystem` locks the jobs on the job lists with the reason.

A new faction's company must be added to `WFFactionLockPolicy.FactionOf` and its name to `wf-faction-lock-played`;
`WFFactionLockTest` fails on a job flying a company it doesn't know.
