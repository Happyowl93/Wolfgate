# BoardingAction

Ways to pin a ship down, replacing the old rule that any powered console within 1536 m blocked a jump. That radius
now sits behind the `wf.ftl_proximity_block` CVar, off by default.

Entry points: `WFFtlDisruptionSystem` (the block itself, shared by everything here), `WFHackHarpoonSystem` (the hack),
`WFNpcDisruptionSystem` (NPC ships) and `WFBoardingHelmSystem` (the helm banner and its release button).

## FTL block

A held ship carries `WFFtlDisruptedComponent`, with one entry for each thing holding it. `CanFTL` refuses it through
`ConsoleFTLAttemptEvent`, `TrySetupFTL` refuses it whatever started the jump, the expedition console refuses it before
generating a mission, and a docked group can't leave while any ship in it is held. A spool already running is stopped
(`ShuttleSystem.WfAbortFtlSpool`).

Stations, protected grids and ships docked to them can't be tackled (`IsTackleable`).

## Hacking harpoon

`WFHackHarpoonGun` is a light hardpoint gun fired from gunnery or the cockpit, built on the Tether module's harpoon
(`ShipHarpoonTurret` with `manned: false`). It is loaded by hand with one `WFHackDart`. A dart that glances, misses or
meets a shield is destroyed, and a hit has to be square on, as with any harpoon.

The cable (`WFRopeHackCable`) pulls on nothing and has sparks running along it. It pays out as the ships move, keeping
a little slack, and parts at 250 m. On the latch:

- The target's FTL drops at once, its helms sound an alarm and show a warning, and blue arcs run across all its
  engines until the last cable is gone, with bursts of sparks.
- After 8 seconds its ship is held in park. Its guns won't fire along any line that crosses the hacker's hull, its
  seekers won't take the hacker, and its guided missiles won't steer onto it. Only the hacker's own ship is protected.
- For as long as the cable is out, no ship gun on the hacker can fire, flares included. Its helm shows a Release button.

Park only raises damping, so a hacked ship can still crawl. Its crew can end the hack by cutting the cable or prying the
dart out of the hull. It also ends if the cable parts, either ship jumps, the gun is destroyed or the dart comes out. A
hack has no time limit unless `maxDuration` is set on the gun.

Both gun rules are one handler on `ShotAttemptedEvent` for `FireControllable` guns, which every gunnery, cockpit, NPC and
signal shot passes through.

## NPC ships

An encounter ship with a gunner, or a hostile drone, disrupts any ship it is firing on
within `wf.npc_disrupt_range` (1500 m by default). Hired crew on a player's ship don't, and encounter ships never hold
each other, since they leave their own fights by jumping.
