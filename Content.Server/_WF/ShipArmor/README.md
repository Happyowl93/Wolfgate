# ShipArmor

Armor-backed mounts. Ship guns and thrusters sit on the hull, so without shields they are the first things a fight
destroys. A mount with `WFArmorBackedComponent` moves part of the damage it takes onto the thickest hull armor within
one tile (any anchored entity with `ArmorThicknessComponent`), so its armor has to be worn through before it dies.

- Explosions move 80%, non-penetrating shell hits 40%, armor-piercing rounds (projectiles with a
  `PenetrationThreshold`) nothing. Hitscan, melee, heat and collisions are untouched.
- The share scales with the armor's thickness up to `FullThickness` (10): a shuttle wall (5) covers half,
  plastitanium (10) all of it.
- Examining a mount shows how much its armor currently covers.

Entry point: `WFArmorBackedSystem`, which cancels `BeforeDamageChangedEvent` and re-applies the split damage.
