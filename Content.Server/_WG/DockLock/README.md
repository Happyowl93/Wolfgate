# DockLock

Per-port dock locks. Each port in the helm's docking screen port list has a PORT LOCK key under its entry, in the
standard console, the combat console and the cockpit alike. Only the ship's crew can press it: its owner or manager
(`WGShipAccessSystem.CanManage`), a card on its allow, crew or company lists, or someone of the same faction as the
grid's company. A locked port (`WGDockLockComponent` on the port) refuses:

- new docking from any helm, autopilot, NPC pilot or FTL arrival: `DockingSystem.CanDock` and the FTL docking
  configurations skip it, and a helm's dock request is refused with a popup saying which port is locked;
- undocking asked for at its own ship's helm, by either port of the joint, with a popup;
- Undock All at its own ship's helm, which undocks the rest and says how many locked ports stayed docked.

A ship docked to a locked port can still cast off from its own helm: the lock is not a clamp. FTL departure,
unanchoring and destruction still break a locked joint.

`WGDockLockSystem` holds the rules and handles `WGDockLockToggleMessage` from the helm. Marked hooks in
`DockingSystem` (dock, undock and Undock All requests, `CanDock`, the FTL docking configurations) and in
`ShuttleConsoleSystem.GetAllDocks` (`DockingPortState.WgDockLocked`, for the screen) call into it. The client
partial `DockingScreen.DockLock.cs` adds the keys and sends the request through the helm the player pilots.

`WGDockLockTest` covers who may lock a port, docking refused from either side, and undock and Undock All refused
from the locked side only.
