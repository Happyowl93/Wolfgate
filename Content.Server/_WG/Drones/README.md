# Drones

Wolfgate changes to the Crescent drone control (`_Crescent/DroneControl`): drones linked to a drone control
console, such as the Fujian's, that fly and fight on its Move and Target orders.

Friendly fire. A drone's guns fire through `ShipTargetingSystem.FireWeapons`; a marked hook there asks
`WGDroneFireGuardSystem.HoldsFire` before each shot. A gun fired by an entity with `DroneControlComponent` holds fire
while the line to its aim point crosses a grid of its own side: the grid of any drone control console the drone is
linked to (its carrier), another drone linked to the same console, or a ship whose company is of the same faction
(`WGCompanyFamily`) as the drone's or its carrier's and that shows its IFF. Its own hull is left to the native firing
check. A gun's line is looked at again every half second. Ships with other masters, NPC crews and player gunners are
not affected.

`WGDroneFireGuardTest` checks a drone against a walled carrier: clear while the carrier is a stranger, fouled once
the carrier's console commands it, fouled by a same-faction hull and clear again when that hull runs dark.
